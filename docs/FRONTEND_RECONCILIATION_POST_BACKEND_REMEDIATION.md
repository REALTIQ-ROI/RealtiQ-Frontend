- Seat-plan item fields are omitted from `plans[]`; no bank-directory endpoint/response is specified for payout setup.
- The staff signatory decision route is documented, but a staff queue/read/detail route is not.
- Import row-correction and existing/new-unit-type confirmation bodies are not fully enumerated.
- Listing-verification upload policy/parser details and complete submission/review DTOs are not all present.
- Staff privacy decision body/version fields remain incomplete.
- Some moderation queue projections and the staff account auth-version projection remain incomplete.
- The no-body trust recompute route has no documented step-up binding representation; the settings read omits an admin revision; notification/virtual-tour external behavior remains provider-gated.
- Ownership transfer/history and dashboard metric definitions remain explicit missing specifications; media entitlement and construction-milestone decisions remain unresolved.
